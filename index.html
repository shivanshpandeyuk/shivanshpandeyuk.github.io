At Cambridge I finished with a first, and 84% in my final year. The faculty gave my dissertation its prize for the best piece of econometrics that year: it looked at the UK's superfast broadband subsidies and found a "copper trap", where subsidies for copper broadband held back the rollout of fibre. I spent summer 2026 on Rothschild & Co / Redburn Atlantic's software and cloud desk, building two stock models from scratch and a live tracker of the datacentre build-out worldwide; before that a $500m software acquisition at Bending Spoons, and infrastructure valuations at EY-Parthenon.
